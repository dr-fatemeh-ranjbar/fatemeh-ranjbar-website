01 / Research Profile

پروفایل پژوهشی

تمرکز پژوهشی من بر بررسی پیوند میان ادبیات، ساختارهای اجتماعی، فرهنگ، هویت و جنسیت قرار دارد.

پژوهش‌های من بر جامعه‌شناسی ادبی و مطالعات جنسیت در ادبیات و جامعه تمرکز دارد. در این مطالعات، متن ادبی نه تنها به عنوان یک اثر زیبایی‌شناختی، بلکه به عنوان بخشی از نظام فرهنگی و اجتماعی مورد بررسی قرار می‌گیرد.

بخش مهمی از این پژوهش‌ها به تحلیل بازنمایی جنسیت، روابط قدرت، هویت‌های اجتماعی، تجربه زنان و تحولات فرهنگی در ادبیات فارسی اختصاص دارد.

01 / Research Profile

Research Profile

My research focuses on the intersections of literature, social structures, culture, identity, and gender.

My research focuses primarily on Literary Sociology and Gender Studies in Literature and Society. I approach literary texts not only as aesthetic works, but also as cultural and social formations shaped by broader historical and institutional contexts.

A significant part of my work examines representations of gender, power relations, social identities, women's experiences, and cultural transformations in Persian literary texts.

02 / Research Areas

Research Areas

Core areas of research focusing on the relationship between literary texts, society, gender, and culture.

AREA 01

Literary Sociology

Studying literature as a social phenomenon and examining the relationships among literary production, social structures, institutions, power, and historical contexts.

Literature & Society Social Structures Power
AREA 02

Gender Studies in Literature and Society

Examining representations of gender, identity, femininity, masculinity, women's experiences, social roles, and power relations in literary and cultural narratives.

Gender Identity Representation
AREA 03

Literature, Culture & Society

Examining the relationships among literary works, cultural meanings, collective memory, social identity, and social transformation.

Cultural Studies Collective Memory Social Change
AREA 04

Literary Criticism & Theory

Applying contemporary literary theories and interdisciplinary approaches to analyze literature in relation to cultural and social questions.

Literary Theory Criticism Interdisciplinary
